Illustrative only, not a guarantee. This tool is for education and uses hypothetical payout, income multiplier and interest assumptions. Actual payout rates, fees, credited interest and income vary by carrier, product, rider and issue date, and index credits are not smooth from year to year. The "runs out" age assumes a constant credited rate and income taken at the start of each year; with a lifetime income rider, payments continue for life after the account value reaches zero, and without one they stop.
